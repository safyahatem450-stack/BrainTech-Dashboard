Guided validation tests (live mode)

–
–

Blink test: blink hard about every 2 s. Eyes test: follow the beeps (high tone = open, low tone = closed), 4 s settle then 26 s measured per phase. A closed/open alpha ratio above 1 across cycles supports real alpha reactivity. The simulation does not model eye closure.

Auditory oddball (P300 estimate)

idle

Needs speakers or headphones. Averages Fp1 epochs (−100 to 600 ms) for rare 1500 Hz tones minus frequent 1000 Hz tones. About 12 rare trials gives a noisy estimate. In simulation, a synthetic P300 is injected for the selected profile.
